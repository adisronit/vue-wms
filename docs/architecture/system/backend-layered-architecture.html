Backend Layered ArchitectureImplemented

The Go backend's internal layering: three cmd/ entrypoints sharing a common platform, messaging, and RBAC layer, and 20 business modules that mostly follow handler → service → repository → model, with two documented exceptions.

Implementation Notes

  • auth has no repository.go of its own — it delegates lookups to user.Repository and role.Repository through narrow interfaces.
  • dashboard and health have no repository either — they aggregate other modules' own services (receiving, picking, inventory) instead of owning a table.
  • Every handler's Register(rg, jwtSecret) wires middleware.RequireJWT(secret) first, then a per-route middleware.RequirePermission(code), before the handler method runs.
  • inventory exposes zero HTTP mutation routes. Six modules — receiving, putaway, picking, replenishment, stockadjustment, stockcount — call its *Tx primitives directly as a shared library, joining the caller's own open GORM transaction.
  • Contrary to a naive reading of the call graph, shipping and order do NOT call inventory directly — picking performs the reserve/consume at task-creation and confirm time instead.
